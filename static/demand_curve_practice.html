The big idea: every point on a demand curve is a best choice. Changing the good's own price moves the consumer along the curve. Changing income, other prices, or tastes shifts the whole curve.

Notation: MRS is the marginal rate of substitution (the slope of the indifference curve); PX and PY are the prices of the horizontal- and vertical-axis goods.
